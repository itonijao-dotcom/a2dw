Questions

Good questions.
Honest answers.

If yours isn't here, just ask — we reply within 2 hours during business hours.

How long until I see results?

+
Paid ads typically show signal within 2–3 weeks. Organic and content compound — expect meaningful lift by days 60–90. We share a live dashboard from day one, so you never have to guess.

Do you work with small budgets?

+
Yes — we start from €500/month and scale with you. What we need is a real product, a clear offer, and willingness to test. We've taken businesses from zero online revenue to consistent daily bookings.

What do you actually report on?

+
ROAS, CPA, revenue generated, cost per lead, funnel conversion rates — the numbers that pay your bills. Likes and reach are context, not KPIs.

Who owns the content you produce?

+
You do. Every asset — raw footage, edited video, ad creative, design files — is yours the moment it's approved. No licensing weirdness, no hostage files.

Can I cancel anytime?

+
Yes. Month-to-month, 30 days notice. We'd rather you stay because the work is good — not because you're locked in.
